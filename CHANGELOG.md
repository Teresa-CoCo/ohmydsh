## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-06

### Added

- Initial release.
- TUI and desktop surfaces over one shared `@deepseek-ai/dsh-base` backend.
- OMP modes: `plan`, `build`, `yolo`.
- Permission-preset and agent-preset layers in the ohmydsh bundle.
- Launcher CLI (`ohmydsh`).
- Composition verifier (`scripts/verify-composition.mjs`).
- Upstream auto-tracking (`scripts/upstream-sync.mjs`,
  `.github/workflows/upstream-watch.yml`).
